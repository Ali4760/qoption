Keep your sign-in details private

When contacting support, share the time, pair, or request status for the issue. Keep your password and verification codes private.

Account access

How do I get started?

Choose Create an account, enter an email address and password, then follow the prompts to open your workspace.

Do I need an invitation to register?

You can create a Qoption account directly from the registration page. All new accounts start with the same workspace features.

What should I do if I can’t sign in?

Check that you’re using the email address you registered with. To reset your password, open Sign in, choose Forgot password?, and follow the link sent to that address.

How is access to my account controlled?

Qoption uses Firebase Authentication. You sign in to view your own wallet and trade history. Staff tools have a separate sign-in and require staff permissions.

Charts and trades

Where are current crypto prices shown?

Open the market preview and pick a pair from the watchlist. The selected pair’s last price is displayed beside the chart.

What steps do I follow to place an order?

In Trading, select an asset, enter a stake in Practice Credits, choose an expiry and direction, and review the details before confirming.

How do I read the chart layers?

Candlesticks show the open, high, low, and close for each interval. The line joins closing prices, and the bars at the bottom show traded volume.

Which crypto pairs can I follow?

Qoption shows BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, ADA, and DOGE against USDT. You can switch between them from the public watchlist or the workspace’s asset picker.

Why are the two charts showing different prices?

The public chart shows live Binance spot data. The order workspace uses its own practice price feed to settle trades made with virtual funds.

Wallet and transfers

How can I add Practice Credits?

Open your Wallet, enter an amount in the deposit form, and submit the request. Approved requests add Practice Credits to your balance.

What happens when I request a withdrawal?

A withdrawal request places the requested Practice Credits on hold. Your wallet shows whether the request is pending, approved, rejected, or cancelled.

Where can I see the review status?

Deposit and withdrawal requests are reviewed by staff. Open the funding history in your wallet to check the latest status.

Tiers and rankings

Is there a membership upgrade?

Qoption currently offers one set of account features, including the trading workspace, wallet, and account history.

What do the Leaderboard rankings mean?

The Leaderboard shows example standings, including sample trade counts and results. Rankings are for illustration and carry no account privileges.

Devices and support

Can I use Qoption on my phone?

Open Qoption in your mobile browser. The charts, account screens, and order controls adapt to smaller displays.

What if the workspace feels stuck while scrolling?

Close any open order or account dialog, then swipe on the page beside the chart. Gestures inside the chart can move the displayed price history.

How do I contact the team?

Sign in and open Messages to send a support request. Include the relevant asset, approximate time, and what you were trying to do.

Where can I explore Qoption’s features?

Visit the homepage for the market preview and an overview of the order workflow, wallet, and trade history.
