Are the prices on this page live?

The crypto chart streams Binance spot prices. The watchlist and statistics refresh periodically. The forex and gold figures are reference quotes.

How do trades settle?

At expiry, the workspace compares the entry price with the live closing market price, then updates your Real Account balance.

Do I need an account to see the charts?

The public chart and watchlist are available here. Sign in to use your wallet, trade history, and support messages.
